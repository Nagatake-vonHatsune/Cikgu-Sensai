**Rules for the Kata-Kata session (from the charter and Sam's decisions):**

- The Malay ledger (Google Sheet; copy in `curriculum/reference/katakata/malay-ledger.csv`) is the source of truth for
  every word, meaning and chapter. Each new card takes the ledger row's ID, Malay, meanings and place. Do not add a word
  that is not in the ledger; propose it to Sam instead.
- Malaysian standard Malay as in Kamus Dewan, never Indonesian. Standard forms first; spoken forms marked "spoken".
- No productive ber- or me- before chapters 10 and 11. No faith-specific greetings anywhere.
- Etymology notes: only Sam's or existing card notes he has not corrected. All Japanese is a draft for native review.
- When a card or frame changes, the ledger's "Kata-Kata (card IDs)" column and the Journal need the same change (Sam or
  a session with sheet access), and Cikgu Sensai's snapshot of `c01.json` to `c09.json` is refreshed afterwards.
- Findings marked **proposal** need Sam's answer before they are done. Everything else is a recorded decision.

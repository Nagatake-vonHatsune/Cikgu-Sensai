@ Move a workshop without losing participants
Change the booking
The venue has just offered a second room. Would that solve the access problem?
Only [if] it has a step-free entrance. Have they confirmed that?
Yes, but the two groups would need separate trainers.
In that case, the room alone doesn’t settle it. Could one group start later?
Possibly. What can we tell the people who have already booked transport?
Confirm their original start, [provided] we can reserve the first room now.
I’ll ring the venue. Will you hold off sending the timetable until I reply?
Yes. I’ll send an interim message explaining when we’ll confirm.

Boleh. Kita catat nombor telefon untuk penduduk yang perlukan bantuan.

@ Brief a committee on an uncertain finding
Revise the public wording
A colleague has sent a press line saying the pilot proved demand. Can we sign it off?
That goes [beyond] the evidence. The trial hasn’t even begun.
Would “learners asked for an earlier start” capture the point?
Some did. Others asked us to retain the existing slot. Both count.
How about saying we’re testing an additional option in response to feedback?
Yes, [provided] the evening class remains available throughout.
Should we promise to keep whichever slot draws the bigger group?
No. Say how the committee will consider people who have no workable alternative.
@ Menimbang dapatan tinjauan kampus

Jika kita sebut ada pelajar meminta perjalanan lewat, adakah itu memadai?
Lebih tepat. Nyatakan juga cadangan masih [tertakluk pada] semakan kos.
Ada yang bimbang kenyataan itu kedengaran seperti alasan untuk bertangguh.
Sertakan tarikh semakan dan pihak yang bertanggungjawab. Itu boleh disahkan.
Jadi kita umumkan proses dan tempoh, tanpa mendahului keputusan?
Ya. Beri juga saluran kepada pelajar yang tidak menjawab tinjauan asal.
@ 調査結果を委員会に伝える

いいえ。困らないという回答が賛成を意味する[とは限りません]。
では、試行に合意したのは利用者ではなく委員会だと明記しますか。
ええ。その際、学生代表が示した条件も落とさないでください。
当日枠を設けること[を前提として]、という点ですね。
そうです。夜間利用者の意見はこれから集めることも伝えましょう。
見出しは「夜間相談の予約制を試行へ」でどうでしょう。
それなら決定の範囲が明確です。本文に見直し日も入れてください。
